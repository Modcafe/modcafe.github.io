# {{ $frontmatter.title }}

Licenses are very important in developing. They can decide what people are allowed to do with your code.

::: tip
If a project does not show a license, it is always licensed under **All right reserved** (short ARR), which means that people are not allowed to do anything with it (except for using).
:::

## Our Licenses
We mostly license our projects under *source-available* or *open-source*. This means that everyone can look at the code. We chose the [IGPL-1.0](https://github.com/intensed-dev/IGPL/blob/0378121bff9429d0fe9bd358bd737aed4dddaabe/IGPL-1.0) for most of your projects. Here is a more detailed list:
